About PIXTION

PIXTION

PIXTION is a guided pixel animation studio that runs in your browser. Draw on grids from 8×8 up to 32×32, build an animation frame by frame, and watch it move in the preview.

Grid sizes
8×8, 12×12, 16×16, 20×20, 24×24, 32×32, 48×48, 64×64, 128×128, 256×256, 512×512, or any custom size from 4 to 512
Tools
Pencil, Eraser, Fill area, Picker, Line, Box, Circle, Filled box, Spray, Dither, Replace colour, Text, Mirror drawing, Brush from 1 to 16 px, Zoom up to 16× with Pan, Undo and Redo
Extras
Turn a picture into pixel art, flip and move frames, gallery search and sort, backup and restore
Animation
Frame strip, Duplicate, Onion skin, speed from 1 to 24 fps, Ping-pong loop
Templates
55 animated samples (including 128×128, 256×256 and 512×512 scenes), 31 starter drawings and scenes, and 11 blank canvas sizes
Export
Animated GIF, video (MP4 or WebM), PNG and sprite sheet
Palette
47 colours, including skin tones and neon shades
Shortcuts

Tutorial

Your first animation in 14 steps

  1. Start blank or from a template

    Open the Templates tab and filter by Animated, Characters, Nature, Objects or Scenes, or pick a blank canvas size.

  2. Pick a grid size

    8×8 suits tiny icons, 16×16 suits characters, 32×32 to 64×64 suit detailed scenes, and 128×128 up to 512×512 are for big pixel paintings. You can also type any custom size from 4 to 512.

  3. Zoom in on big grids

    Use Zoom 2× to 16×, then ✋ Pan to move around. Bigger brushes (4, 8 or 16 px) fill large areas faster on 128×128 and up.

  4. Choose a colour and draw

    Tap a colour in the Palette, then tap or drag on the grid with the Pencil. Eraser removes pixels and Fill area paints a whole region at once.

  5. Use the shape tools

    Line, Box, Circle and Filled draw shapes as you drag. Spray scatters pixels, Dither paints a checker pattern for shading, Replace swaps one colour for another everywhere, and Text stamps words in a pixel font (Brush size sets the letter size). Picker copies a colour, and Mirror drawing copies every stroke to the other side.

  6. Made a mistake? Undo it

    Undo and Redo are in the Tools box. They also bring back frames you cleared or deleted.

  7. Start from a picture

    Tap "Turn a picture into pixel art" and choose a photo. PIXTION shrinks it to your grid using the palette colours, and you can tidy it up by hand.

  8. Move and flip

    Transform flips the frame or shifts it one pixel at a time. Turn on "Apply to every frame" to move the whole animation.

  9. Duplicate the frame

    Tap Duplicate to copy your drawing into a new frame. Small changes between copies are what make things move.

  10. Turn on Onion skin

    The previous frame shows faintly behind the current one, so you can line up each step of the movement.

  11. Play the preview

    Tap Play and adjust Speed. 6 to 10 frames per second suits most pixel animations. Ping-pong loop plays forward, then backward, which looks smooth for breathing and swinging.

  12. Save your art

    Tap Save art and give it a title and an optional description. Open it later from the Gallery to play it or keep editing.

  13. Download and share

    In the Gallery, open a piece and choose GIF, video, PNG or sprite sheet. If you opened PIXTION from Messenger, open it in your browser first so the download works.

  14. Back up your gallery

    Your art lives in this browser. In Settings, tap "Back up my art" to save one file you can restore on any device.
